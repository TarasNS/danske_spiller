## Ambiguous remainders (tense cue not unambiguous)
1. Present template #43 `ligge`: "Byen [v] nu tæt på kysten." The only cue is bare "nu", which also fits past narrative ("Byen lå nu tæt på kysten"); both "ligger" and "lå" are therefore defensible. The datid "lå" can appear as a distractor for this item, so the item can have two defensible answers. Recommend "lige nu" or "i dag". The safety-net regex also treats bare "nu" as a present cue, so it would not protect this.
2. Present template #74 `huske`: "Jeg [v] hendes fødselsdag og køber en gave nu." Disambiguated by the fixed present "køber" in the same sentence, so only "husker" is grammatical; content is odd (UNCERTAIN, below), not tense-ambiguous.
3. Pre-existing distractor risk, not part of the story: generated filler forms `sovede`, `liggede`, `talede`, `spørgede`, `tænkede`, `hjælpede` etc. are real colloquial/non-standard variants of the past tense (sov, lå, talte, spurgte, tænkte, hjalp). They can appear as wrong options next to the standard form. Unchanged since HEAD; flag for the native reviewer.
No other present or past template has a second valid answer among the options.
