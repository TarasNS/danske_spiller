## 1. Implementation summary
- QA-100: "Gå op i en højere enhed" rewritten: m "To come together perfectly / form a harmonious whole", x "For different parts to fit together into a harmonious whole.", e "Musikken og billederne gik op i en højere enhed.", t "The music and the images came together as a harmonious whole."; category changed to "Everyday Life" (level C1, id and k unchanged).
- QA-101: deleted "Have rejst sig på den forkerte side" (calque; "Stå op med det forkerte ben først" remains).
- QA-102: exact duplicates merged by deleting the variants "Slå hovedet på sømmet" (kept "Ramme hovedet på sømmet") and "Det er ikke nogen sag" (kept "Det er ingen sag"). New optional field `g` (synonym group) on: rammeplet (Ramme plet, Ramme hovedet på sømmet, Være lige i skabet), fejle (Gå i vasken, Gå i fisk), sigedet (Tale lige ud af posen, Sige rent ud, Tage bladet fra munden), plus two extra groups I judged the same kind of problem: glat (Det går som smurt, Det kører på skinner), forkert (Det er hen i vejret, Være ude i hampen). `otherMeanings`/`otherIdioms` skip every member of the answer's group (`sameGroup`).
- Unknown stored keys: `load()` now normalises `idiom`/`ach`; the "Idiomopdager" achievement and the stats "mødt" count only count keys present in `DATA_BY_ID`. `masteredCount`, per-category/level stats and weak lists already iterate `DATA`, so orphan keys are ignored. Ids are derived from `d`, so nothing else was re-keyed; stored progress for the 3 deleted idioms stays harmlessly in localStorage.
- Data count 171 -> 168; no duplicate ids or duplicate `d`.
