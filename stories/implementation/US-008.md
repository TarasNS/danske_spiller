# US-008 — Idiomjæger: Modersmålstaleren at A2/B1 (worker W-IDIOM)

## 1. Implementation summary
- `launchGame('native')`: if the level-filtered B2/C1 pool is empty (A2/B1 filter), it falls back to all B2/C1 idioms and shows the Danish toast "Modersmålstaleren er kun for B2–C1 – spiller med alle B2–C1-idiomer."
- General guard: new `emptyPool()` (renders the menu + toast "Ingen idiomer matcher dit valg. Vælg et andet niveau.") used by `startQuiz`, `nextQuestion`, `startText` (after the `canUse` filter). `startMemory`/`startMatch` fall back to all idioms with a toast when the level pool has fewer than 6/5 items.
- "Alle niveauer" behaviour unchanged.
