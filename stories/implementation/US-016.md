## 1. Implementation summary
- QA-091: `rejse` aux changed `er` -> `har`, template now "Vi har [v] til Spanien flere gange i år." `begynde` aux changed `har` -> `er`, template "Filmen er [v] uden mig." - QA-092, anchoring (chosen over widening the key):
  - 18 past templates (`d`) rewritten so each has an explicit past cue: the 14 non-temporal openers ("Til festen", "På mødet", "På ferien", "På projektet", "Til eksamen", "Til timen", "Til koncerten", "Til foredraget", "Til fødselsdagen") moved to "I lørdags / I går / Sidste sommer / Sidste år / I sidste uge ... [v] ..." with the old place phrase moved to the end; plus 4 weak cues fixed ("I weekenden" x3 -> "I sidste weekend", "I nat" -> "I går ... hele natten", because both can also be present/future).
  - All 78 present templates (`p`) rewritten with a cue that excludes the past: "lige nu", "i øjeblikket", "i morgen", "næste uge/år/sommer/søndag", "om fem minutter / en halv time / to år". Habitual cues ("hver dag", "altid", "om morgenen") were deliberately NOT used because they also allow past habitual ("Jeg drak kaffe hver morgen"). English glosses (`en`) updated to match (progressive/future).
  - Safety net: `PAST_CUE` / `PRES_CUE` regexes + `hasPastCue/hasPresCue`; `buildOptions(correct, verbKey, exclude)` now leaves out the competing tense form in arena/timemachine/repair questions when the sentence has no cue (never triggers on current data, guards future entries).
  - Arena explanation "Tidsudtrykket peger på fortiden." is now appended only when the template has a past cue.
- IDs, storage key (`magiske_verber_v1`), question count (735), data shapes, OK_MSGS/NO_MSGS unchanged.
